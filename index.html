About Rooftop Urban Agro Park

Building a Sustainable, Green, and Smart Future Through Urban Farming Innovation

Located in Indore, Rooftop Urban Agro Park is your one-stop plant nursery and urban farming partner. We blend technology, horticulture, and nature to transform unused residential, commercial, and institutional spaces into thriving green ecosystems.

Our Vision

Empower citizens to grow their own food and support greener communities.

Our Mission

Make urban farming easy, accessible & sustainable for every home.
